vendor updates
don't take you down

"what happens when akamai changes something?" is the question everyone asks. the answer is that our tooling discovers the change and patches the solver — that loop is the product.

our tooling finds the change
we watch the live challenges continuously. when a vendor ships new logic, the tooling auto-discovers what changed — we're not waiting on your bug report.
it patches the solver
the fix is generated and tested automatically, then a new image goes to your private registry. you pull it and carry on.
built to survive minor updates
we solve the challenge vm itself, not individual scripts, so most vendor changes never reach you in the first place.

nothing leaves
your network

every other option means shipping your customers' requests, cookies and tokens to someone else's api — the thing enterprise security reviews stop. xhr.dev runs on your side of the boundary, and works with outbound internet turned off entirely.
